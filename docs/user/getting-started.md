# Getting started

Talaria signs you in with Google — there are no passwords to make and no profile to fill
in. Your admin invites you by email; the first thing you land on is your **Inbox**, where
your own assistant writes you a daily brief. This page takes you from the invite to knowing
where everything lives.

## Sign in

1. Open the join link your admin sent, or your workspace's address.
2. **Continue with Google** — use the email you were invited with; the invite is bound to
   it, so a different account won't use it.
3. You're in. Invites expire after two weeks — ask for a fresh one if yours has.

Trouble: "This invite is no longer valid" means it expired or was revoked — your admin can
re-issue. If Google isn't offered, your workspace may not have it enabled yet; ask your
admin.

## Set up your assistant (do this first)

Your assistant is yours alone — its own memory, skills, and private knowledge, running on a
model nobody else picked, because it reads your private work.

1. **Set up your assistant** — the card at the top of your sidebar, or **Settings → Assistant**.
2. **Name** it and give it a **handle** — the handle is how agents and integrations refer to
   it, and can't be changed later.
3. Pick a personality preset (Warm & proactive · Concise & professional · Playful & curious)
   and optionally add a line of your own.
4. **Create assistant** — its workspace starts up, and your first daily brief opens tomorrow
   morning (it arrives two hours before your workday starts, so it's waiting for you).

## Find your way around

The sidebar has two sections; Settings and Admin live in your user menu (top right).

| Section | Item | What it is |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Work** | **Inbox** | Your day: the daily brief, and one-screen digests of everything waiting |
| | **Comms** | Every conversation — channels, relays, DMs, agent chats |
| | **Plan** | Think work through with an agent, then turn it into tickets |
| | **Boards** | The team's tracked work — tickets people and agents move to done |
| | **Research** | Cited research reports, run by your agents |
| | **Knowledge** | What the org knows — documents agents can cite |
| | **Files** | Documents and uploads, including everything agents produce |
| **Manage** | Agents, Models, MCP, Templates, Agent Studio, Observability, Apps | The control plane — visible only if an admin granted it to you |

Which Work and Manage items you see is set per person by your admin (Admin → People).

## Make it yours

| To… | Where |
| :--- | :--- |
| Change your name / how you appear | **Settings → Profile** |
| Tune notifications (per class: in-app, email, or both) | **Settings → Notifications** — plus the **Daily digest**, one email a day only when something waits |
| Connect your Google account (mail, calendar, Drive for your assistant) | **Settings → Connections** |
| Create an API key | **Settings → API keys** |

Connecting Google is what lets your assistant see your mail and calendar — it acts as you,
and outbound mail and invites always wait for your approval.
