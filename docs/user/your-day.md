# Your day

The **Inbox** (Work → Inbox) is where your day starts: your assistant's daily brief — what
needs you, what's on today, who's waiting on you — with the rest of your work one screen
down. Your assistant writes the brief from your private work each morning, appends to it
through the day, and never rewrites it. Beside it, the assistant drawer is where decisions
get made without leaving the page.

## The daily brief

Four sections, each with an open count:

| Section | What lands there |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Needs you** | Work that has stopped and is waiting on a decision from you — approvals, tickets to triage, review, or unblock |
| **Today** | Your calendar for today |
| **Waiting on you** | People who asked you something and have not heard back |
| **Worth knowing** | Nothing is blocked on these; they are just worth knowing |

The header says who wrote it and when it opened. Handle a line with its actions — **Check
this off**, **Dismiss**, or **Ask your assistant about this** — and resolved lines stay on
the page, struck through. **N new since you looked** (or **Timeline**) shows what was
appended.

The **Mail** and **Agenda** buttons open live Google panes beside the brief (once your
account is connected in Settings → Connections) — they are real windows into your inbox and
calendar, not part of the brief. **Open in Files** opens the brief itself as a document.

No assistant yet? The brief says so, with a **Set up your assistant** button — see
[getting started](./getting-started.md).

## Below the brief: your queues

| Tab | What it shows |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Boards** | Your queues: **To triage** (new tickets unassigned), **To review** (agent work awaiting your sign-off), **Blocked** (stalled, needs you) |
| **Comms** | Unread messages and channel activity |
| **Plans** / **Research** / **Docs** | Your recent plans, research runs, and updated documents |

## The assistant drawer

Press **Ctrl/Cmd + \\** (or click your assistant in the sidebar) from anywhere.

- The conversation follows you across Talaria and answers with the tools that match the
  view you're on.
- When you arrive from a decision — a brief line, a waiting approval — it's **attached**:
  your instructions execute against that item. The paperclip detaches it for a general chat.
- Risky moves always come back as a **Confirm exact action** card before anything happens;
  anything a model proposed needs your confirmation, even when the action itself is safe.
  The timeline shows what was proposed, confirmed, completed, undone.
- Queue items can be **snoozed** — 1 hour, tomorrow, next week.

One action runs at a time; if your assistant is mid-action you'll hear "Your assistant is
already handling another Inbox action."

## Notifications

Every notification is kept in your inbox — the **bell** at the top right. Its count is what's
unread; open it and the latest land on top. Clicking a row takes you there and marks it read;
**Mark all read** clears the count. What reaches you as a toast or an email is per
class (**Settings → Notifications**):

| Class | About | Default |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Mentions & shares | @mentions, ticket assignments, shared docs | Both |
| Direct messages | Teammates and agents, DMing you | Both |
| Approvals waiting on you | Work parked until you approve or reject | Both |
| Judge escalations | The quality gate stopped work and handed it to a person | Both |
| Blocked agents | An agent hit a problem and stopped until someone looks | Both |
| Capability gaps | An agent reported something it couldn't do | In-app |
| Work finished | A ticket moved, or research is ready | In-app |

**Daily digest**: one email a day listing what's waiting — never sent on a day when
nothing is.
