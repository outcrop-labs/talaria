- **Home's Inbox is one page now: a Today card, Messages, and a Needs you / Running / Done today board, with a side drawer for any card.** The Boards, Comms, Work and Docs sub-tabs are gone, and the board shows their work. `/home/boards`, `/home/comms`, `/home/work` and `/home/docs` redirect to `/home/inbox`. The Today card shows the date, up to two lines of the brief, the next meeting, and one button for each kind of work that needs you (Blocked, Awaiting sign-off, Triage). A button scrolls to its cards. Messages lists the people who wait on a reply, newest first, two rows at a time, each with Mark handled and Reply. The board has three lanes and a Board / List switch that the browser remembers. A board area narrower than 1100px shows the list. A click on a card or a row opens a 520px drawer beside the board. The drawer has the card's actions, a "Why it's blocked" callout on a blocked or failed card, the card's properties, sub-tasks and activity. The drawer goes full screen, and Esc closes it. Mark handled moves a card to Done today and leaves its ticket as it is. Remove from inbox is in the ⋯ menu. Every card shows its owner. Before this change a ticket's owner never appeared, because the board's ticket query did not update after its data arrived. `ui/src/lib/inbox-board.ts` builds the board from the brief's decisions, the queued tickets in `/api/home`, work sessions, research runs and today's documents, with one adapter per source. The Inbox's buttons speak sentence case in the sans face through a new opt-in `voice="sans"` on `Button` and `Segmented`. `Segmented` also gains `variant="solid"`. Their defaults are unchanged. The light theme's `--theme-danger` is one step darker (`#b8480c`), so it meets AA as text on paper and panel. "Open in Files" moved to the Timeline panel's header. Drag and drop between lanes and assigning a card to an agent are not part of this change.
