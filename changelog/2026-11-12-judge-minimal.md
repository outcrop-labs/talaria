- **`judge (review)` is now one bug-hunting read of the diff: no repository docs, no full files, no callers and no blind second reading.** Calibration showed the old reviewer finding almost nothing. It sent `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` about 200k input tokens a review (94 KB of rule docs, the diff and every changed file in full, importers and cross-references), then let a second "verify" call discard whatever it could not confirm. It hit 0 of the 7 dev-set known bugs and 0 of the 3 held-out ones, and listed 3 Tier A findings in 57 reviews. A minimal version on the `judge-calibrate/flash-minimal` experiment branch (run 43184195389) hit 2 of 7: #700's compare-and-set hold, and #686's decide-before-lock, which it anchored 22 lines from the defect's range and was graded by hand. It hit 0 of 3 held-out and listed 19 Tier A findings in 54 reviews. Read against the code, 10 were real and 9 false. 8 of the real ones are still on testing: a major zod alias self-import in sdk-kit (#649), and minors in agent-design, web-search, cleanup-sweep, targetseed, work_status, TabBar focus and omapak-submit. It read about 90k input tokens on #682, at a median $0.0125 a review. This change ships that version without the experiment's switched-off code.

  The model now gets a one-page prompt that asks for the bugs a change would ship and names the three absolute rules. Then come the pull request's header, body and changelog entry, judge-pr's findings, the earlier findings and their answers, the FILES list and the diff at `-U25`. All of it fits a 120k-token budget, down from 300k. A change that does not fit drops to `-U10`, then loses whole files, lowest risk first. Removed from `scripts/judge-review.mjs`: the RULES and REFERENCE docs and their path tables, cross-references and importers, the numbered full files, and their trims. The verify pass is gone too, with its schema, its prompt section, the blind candidate list, the verdict combining, its calls and `--no-verify`. Findings no longer carry `verified` or `confirmedBy`. Tier A is the top three unanswered blockers and majors in a defect category. Under `JUDGE_REVIEW_GATE=blockers`, pr-watch now holds on a Tier A, high-confidence blocker in a gating category, with no second reading to confirm it. Dead-wiring no longer gates, because its gate needed the computed fact that a removed call had no use left. A repo-rule finding must name one of the three rules or cite a changed file. The script still accepts `--rules-from` and ignores it, because the copy of `judge-review.yml` on every open pull request's head still passes it. Unchanged: the model, its price caps and provider filters, the keys, report mode, the trust boundaries, the wire that pr-watch and remote-gate read, the comment's shape and the calibration harness. pr-watch, the PR comment, the skills and docs/AGENT-TOOLING.md no longer say a finding was confirmed.
