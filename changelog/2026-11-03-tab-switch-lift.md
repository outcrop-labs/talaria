- **Clicking a tile in the tab bar lifts the new tab and its view up out of the bar.** The tab rises 8px from 92% scale, the view rises 12px out of a 3px blur 40ms behind it, and the old view keeps rising 8px as it fades, all on one 280ms quint ease-out (160ms for the exit). It plays only on a tile click: a deep link, the back button or a link inside a view switches with no motion, and a click mid-switch starts the next one cleanly. Under reduced motion it is a 150ms opacity crossfade. Pressing a tile now dips it to 97% for 150ms (no dip under reduced motion).
  Verified: `bun run gate`, and `bun run test -- tab-motion` (reduced motion is opacity-only keyframes at 150ms; a click lifts only once its tile opens; a back or deep-link change and a modified click do not lift).
