- **Clicking a tile in the tab bar lifts the new tab and its view up out of the bar.** The tab rises 8px from 92% scale, the view rises 12px out of a 3px blur 40ms behind it, and the old view keeps rising 8px as it fades, all on one 280ms quint ease-out (160ms for the exit). It plays only on a tile click: a deep link, the back button or a link inside a view switches with no motion, and a click mid-switch starts the next one cleanly. Under reduced motion it is a 150ms opacity crossfade. Pressing a tile now dips it to 97% for 150ms (no dip under reduced motion). The fading copy of the old view keeps its scroll position and never loads a video or an app's iframe a second time.
  Verified: `bun run gate`, and `bun run test -- tab-motion` (reduced motion is opacity-only keyframes at 150ms; a click lifts only once its tile opens, also through a redirect inside its route; a back or deep-link change, a modified click, and a click overtaken by a route change elsewhere do not lift). In the running app, against rc on the same stack and data: Work to Boards lifts at head and switches with no motion on rc; five clicks 60ms apart end on the last one with no leftover copy; back, forward and a deep link play nothing; reduced motion is opacity only; at 420px the copy stays inside the viewport; a member with no grants gets the lift and no gear; a scrolled Boards column keeps its offset in the fading copy and the arriving view's scroll matches rc; a video in the old view is fetched once. Click to first paint over 20 switches: 9.4 ms median against rc's 9.5 ms, and 3.45 long frames per switch against rc's 3.40.
