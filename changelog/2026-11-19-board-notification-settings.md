- **Each board can have its own notification setting — part 1, the server: the store, the resolver, and the ticket producers that obey it.** A person picks, board by board, All activity, Participating, Mentions & assignments, Off, or a custom set of events (new ticket, assigned to me, status change, comment, mention, completed) over the tickets they are in or the whole board. A board with no setting follows the person's default for every board (`boards` in `PATCH /api/notifications`), and with no default, Participating — exactly the audiences every ticket producer had before, so nobody hears anything new, or less, until they choose. Every ticket producer asks it once per event, at fan-out: the status move, the review decision, the board move, creation with its assignment, the comment fan-out and the four ticket @mention doors. A ticket a person watches themselves still reaches them on a board set to Off (the watch route now records who added a watch, and pressing Watch on one an editor added makes it yours); approvals always do. All files one counting `board-activity` row per board for the tickets you are not in, in a new matrix row, Board activity, that is quiet by default — never louder than the status moves of your own tickets. A setting changes only what that person receives: `scripts/check-invariants.mjs` confines the setting to the per-recipient fan-outs. New routes: `GET`/`PUT`/`DELETE /api/boards/{id}/notify` (any board role, the caller's own row only) and `GET /api/notifications/boards`; feature `board-prefs`. The board's own control and the Settings section are the next part.
  Verified: CI is the compile and the proof — this PR's checks run the resolver's every-case table (every event × every preset × every candidate shape × both cadences, plus the golden test that the built-in reproduces today's audiences and files nothing new), the route and prefs probe tables, and a new live module, `board_notify_live`, through the real router: with nobody's setting stored the assignee hears the assignment, move, comment and completion, the mentioned person the mention, and a member in none of it nothing; Off silences one person and nobody else, a self-made watch still reaches them and an editor-added one does not until they press Watch; All files one row counting four events, quiet, and a fresh row after a read; a custom set without mentions hears the @mentioning comment; the setting survives leaving and rejoining; a team-leg member sets and obeys one. The existing live notification modules run unchanged.
