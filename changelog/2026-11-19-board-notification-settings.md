- **Each board can have its own notification setting — part 1, the server: the store, the resolver, and the ticket producers that obey it.** A person picks, board by board, All activity, Participating, Mentions & assignments, Off, or a custom set of events (new ticket, assigned to me, status change, comment, mention, completed) over the tickets they are in or the whole board. A board with no setting follows the person's default for every board (`boards` in `PATCH /api/notifications`), and with no default, Participating — exactly the audiences every ticket producer had before, so nobody hears anything new, or less, until they choose. Every ticket producer asks it once per event, at fan-out: the status move, the review decision, the board move, creation with its assignment, the comment fan-out and the four ticket @mention doors. A ticket a person watches themselves still reaches them on a board set to Off (the watch route now records who added a watch, and pressing Watch on one an editor added makes it yours); approvals always do. All files one counting `board-activity` row per board for the tickets you are not in, in a new matrix row, Board activity, that is quiet by default — never louder than the status moves of your own tickets — and that Talaria Desktop posts at low urgency, like a status move. The Board activity row shows in Settings → Notifications as soon as this ships, ahead of the board control (part 3) that sets a board to hear every ticket; until then only the API sets one. One action is one row: a new ticket's "New ticket" board row skips whoever its assignment or its description's @mention reached, and a patch that assigns and moves someone whose setting drops assignments files one status row. A setting changes only what that person receives: `scripts/check-invariants.mjs` confines the setting to the per-recipient fan-outs, by every spelling of its module path (the full path, a braced import, an alias or glob of the crate, and `crate::`, `super::` or a bare `boards::` inside talaria-notify, where the send-time readers live). New routes: `GET`/`PUT`/`DELETE /api/boards/{id}/notify` (any board role, the caller's own row only) and `GET /api/notifications/boards`; feature `board-prefs`. A ClickUp sync's writes stay quiet for everyone, people who hear the whole board included: they file no board-activity row and no per-ticket row, since ClickUp already told its own people. An agent's move out of review on a person's ask is a status move like any other, heard by each person's setting. The board's own control and the Settings section are the next part.
