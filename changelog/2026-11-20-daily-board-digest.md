- **A board can be heard as a daily digest — per-board notification settings, part 5 of 5.** The `digest` cadence of a board's notification setting is a choice now (it was listed but refused): a board set to a daily digest files nothing as things happen — an assignment, an @mention and the news of a ticket you watch yourself still come at once — and once a day, at the daily digest's hour in your own time zone, files one notification per board rolling up what your setting covers since your last digest: new tickets (for a setting over the whole board; a ticket moved here counts once, from its arrival), tickets moved, tickets completed and comments, like "3 new · 8 moved · 2 completed · 11 comments". When due-date reminders ship, due-soon and overdue tickets join the line as thresholds crossed since your last digest — a standing overdue backlog is never news on its own. Your own actions, a ClickUp sync's writes, archived tickets and the news of a ticket you watch yourself are never counted, and only boards you can still see are. The notification is quiet (a digest never rings), one per board per day — a day you never open keeps its own counts rather than being overwritten by the next — and stays quiet whatever the board's Alerts say. The boards whose Email is on — the board's own Email where your setting for it chose one (part 2), else Board activity's — come in one "Your boards" email (never while email is set to Never), held like all notification mail when the workspace's email is off. A person with no email address still gets the notifications. Nothing to report, nothing sent. A new `board-digest` scheduler job (required) runs it on the daily digest's 15-minute tick inside its two-hour window, with a per-person ledger (`board_digest_state`) whose day and mail are each claimed by a compare-and-set: a day is marked filing until its rows land and is never mailed before then; a pass that dies mid-filing leaves a claim the next tick takes over, filing only the rows still missing; a failed or held mail is sent on a later tick without filing again. Feature `board-digest`. The board's own control for choosing it is part 3.
