- **A board can be heard as a daily digest — per-board notification settings, part 5 of 5.** The `digest` cadence of a board's notification setting is a choice now (it was listed but refused): a board set to a daily digest files nothing as things happen — an assignment, an @mention and the news of a ticket you watch yourself still come at once — and once a day, at the daily digest's hour in your own time zone, files one notification per board rolling up what your setting covers since your last digest: new tickets (for a setting over the whole board), tickets moved, tickets completed and comments, like "3 new · 8 moved · 2 completed · 11 comments". Due-soon and overdue tickets join the line when due-date reminders ship. Your own actions, a ClickUp sync's writes, archived tickets and the moves of a ticket you watch yourself are never counted, and only boards you can still see are. The notification is quiet (a digest never rings) and a newer one replaces an unread one; with Board activity's Email on (and email not set to Never) the same boards come in one "Your boards" email, held like all notification mail when the workspace's email is off. Nothing to report, nothing sent. A new `board-digest` scheduler job (required) runs it on the daily digest's 15-minute tick inside its two-hour window, with a per-person ledger (`board_digest_state`) so a second tick never files twice and a failed or held mail is sent on a later tick without filing again; feature `board-digest`. The board's own control for choosing it is part 3.
