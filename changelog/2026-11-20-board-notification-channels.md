- **A board's notification setting can say how its notifications reach you, part 2 of 5: per-board Alerts and Email.** Beside what you hear about a board, your setting for it (and your default for every board) can now set two channels: Alerts — the banner and the push, together — and Email, each on, off, or as Settings → Notifications says (the default, so nothing changes for anyone until they choose). One board's tickets can reach you by mail alone while another's pop up; the inbox keeps every notification either way, so in-app is not a choice (`inApp: false` is refused, as in the matrix), and push is not a board choice of its own. The choice changes only how a notification reaches you, never who gets one, and applies to every row the board files for you: assignments, moves, comments, @mentions, the board-activity row, and the rows a ticket you watch yourself carries through Off. It is decided once, when the notification is filed, and stored on the notification beside the delivery it shaped (as the matrix's own Desktop, Push and Email: Alerts sets the first two together), so the deferred push and the missed-activity mail re-check your matrix through the same choice and never re-read a board setting. DND, working hours, the workspace switches and your email frequency still apply after it. The setting's `channels: {alerts, email}` member is served and written by `GET`/`PUT /api/boards/{id}/notify`, `GET /api/notifications/boards` and `boards` in `PATCH /api/notifications`, behind the new feature `board-channels`; the board's own control and the Settings section are part 3.
  Verified: CI is the compile and the proof — this PR's checks. Unit: the channel overlay for every override value against every matrix value, and the matrix untouched for every class with none; Alerts mapping to Desktop and Push together in both directions; the write decision for each channel widened and narrowed, with DND, the workspace switches, a producer's mute, skip_push and the email frequency still winning and the legacy route read from the channels that applied; the deferred push's and the batcher's send-time re-checks through the row's override (a push or mail a board turned on stays on when the matrix is off, one it turned off stays off, the email-only read rule follows it); the setting's channels through the stored shape, the wire and the write's checks (each message pinned, Desktop and Push dropped as unknown members), and that they never change who gets a row; each person's channels kept apart in the ticket fan-out and the mention door. Live (`board_notify_live`, through the real router, with the instance's mail switch on): one person's board set to Alerts off and Email on files their assignment, move and @mention rows as feed rows carrying that override with the mail armed, although their matrix mails none of those classes; another's board set to Alerts on makes its quiet board-activity row banner; a person whose board is Off with mail only gets the rows their own watch carries by mail, not on screen; a person with no setting gets every row on the matrix with no override and no mail; `inApp: false` is refused; `board-channels` is served; a PUT without channels gives the matrix back. Exercised locally on an earlier revision of this change (separate Desktop and Push choices; the row override, the writer and the send-time re-checks are unchanged since), on CI's api binary against an isolated scratch Postgres and Redis with minted sessions, curl, psql and a loopback SMTP sink: a person's board set to email with no screen got feed rows armed for mail, the batcher kept them after another person turned their own comment mail off and dropped hers, and with the sink as provider an email-only row was filed read once mailed while a screen person's stayed unread.
