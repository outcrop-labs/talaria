- **A board's notification setting can say how its notifications reach you, part 2 of 5: per-board Desktop, Push and Email.** Beside what you hear about a board, your setting for it (and your default for every board) can now set each of the matrix's own channels: Desktop, Push and Email, each on, off, or as Settings → Notifications says (the default, so nothing changes for anyone until they choose). One board's tickets can reach you by mail alone while another's pop up; the inbox keeps every notification either way, so in-app is not a choice (`inApp: false` is refused, as in the matrix). The choice changes only how a notification reaches you, never who gets one, and applies to every row the board files for you: assignments, moves, comments, @mentions, the board-activity row, and the rows a ticket you watch yourself carries through Off. It is decided once, when the notification is filed, and stored on the notification beside the delivery it shaped, so the deferred push and the missed-activity mail re-check your matrix through the same choice and never re-read a board setting. DND, working hours, the workspace switches and your email frequency still apply after it. The setting's `channels` member is served and written by `GET`/`PUT /api/boards/{id}/notify`, `GET /api/notifications/boards` and `boards` in `PATCH /api/notifications`, behind the new feature `board-channels`; the board's own control and the Settings section are part 3.
