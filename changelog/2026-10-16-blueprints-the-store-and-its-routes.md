- **Added:** Blueprints — the asks somebody sends again — get a table and an api. A blueprint is a named piece of text a person keeps and pastes into the composer: `blueprints(name, body, owner_user_id, surface, sort)`, where a NULL owner is the whole access model in one column. A row with an owner is that person's and nobody else lists it; a row without one is the org's, readable by every member and writable only with `templates.manage`, the permission that already governs the org-wide skeletons everyone starts from. `GET /api/blueprints` answers the caller's own plus the shared ones, ordered `sort` then name; `POST` creates one (personal with a session alone, shared with the permission); `PUT` edits — including crossing the line in either direction — and `DELETE` removes. Somebody else's personal blueprint answers 404 and never 403, because a row you cannot see does not exist and a 403 on an id would confirm it is real; a shared row refuses with 403, because everybody can see it and what they are missing is a permission. Verified: `cargo test -p talaria-api --test it blueprints_live:: -- --ignored` 4 passed (a personal blueprint created, listed in sort-then-name order with its camelCase wire shape and its bounds refused at 400; a stranger's list without it and 404 on PUT/DELETE of it; a shared create refused at 403 without the permission, written and read org-wide with it, and refused at 403 to the member who may not edit it; delete gone from the list and from the table), run against a scratch postgres:16-alpine the migration pass had just built; `cargo test -p talaria-routes-knowledge` 7 passed, clippy `-D warnings` clean, `cargo fmt`; `bun scripts/check-migrations.ts --update-snapshot` applied 441/441 with the schema snapshot regenerated; `bun run docs:api` and `bun run check`.
