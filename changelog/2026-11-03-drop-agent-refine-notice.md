- **A document's version history shows every change, an agent's included, and nothing an agent saves can be overwritten by a person's open editor without them knowing.** The **Version history** panel (the ⋯ menu on a KB doc, a space or a file) lists every revision with who saved it (an agent by its name) and when. Opening one shows a **Changes** diff against the version before it, the **Version** itself, and **Restore**. Restore is shown only to people who can edit, and a restore that fails says why in the dialog, which stays open. Files no longer restore on a single click. A document or page open in Read follows the server, so an agent's edit appears without a reload and without any banner. When an agent (or another person) saves a document while you have it open in Edit, your next save is refused instead of replacing their work: one line appears at that moment, "Not saved: this document changed while you were editing", with **Review in version history** and **Keep mine**. A change that only moved the document's metadata (an icon, a move, a promote) never shows the line, and someone else's rename is never undone by your save. What you type after a refusal is kept: leaving the document (another doc, a link, Back, closing the tab, or switching the Work view's file pane to comments) asks first, and the edits stay on this device and are offered back, with **Keep mine** and **Discard**, the next time you open the document. Reading a document and accepting a Muse draft written before an agent changed it is refused the same way, and the draft comes back. Accepting a Muse draft folds into a one-line receipt of what changed (±lines), and the Draft button pulses while Muse writes. On the API, `PUT /api/kb/docs/{id}` and `PUT /api/artifacts/{id}` take `baseUpdatedAt`: the title and body land in one conditional update, or the route answers 409 with the current document. Their GETs answer `canEdit`.
