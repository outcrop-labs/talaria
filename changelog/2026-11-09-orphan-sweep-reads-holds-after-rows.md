- **Fixed: the orphan sweep can no longer close a reply that this process is still writing. It now reads which replies this process holds after it reads the rows, not before.** The sweep built its one statement with this process's holds already read, and the statement read the rows only when it ran. A reply held between the two, and quiet past the lease by the time the statement ran, was in neither list. The sweep closed it as an orphan (`error`, "this reply stopped being saved partway through") while its writer still held it. The sweep now reads the quiet replies first, drops the ones this process holds, and closes the rest with the same guarded close. That is the order `reply_fate` and `active_streaming_assistant` already used. In production a reply is held as soon as it is made, while it is fresh, so the window needed a sweep to stall for most of the two-minute lease. It showed in CI instead: `orphan_live::lease::a_reply_this_process_writes_is_never_its_orphan` failed on #712 and on rc at d9023155 (`orphan_live.rs:196`, `left: None`), because a sibling test's sweep took the row the test held.

  Verified: reproduced on rc d3aedf96 against a worktree Postgres migrated the way CI's live-tests job migrates it. The old code failed 172 of 1,700 suite runs, one process at a time: the crate's ignored tests at CI's four threads failed 8 of 300 (7 of them CI's own `orphan_live.rs:196`); `orphan_live` alone failed 50 of 500 at four threads, 37 of 300 at eight, 44 of 300 pinned to two CPUs, and 33 of 300 at the default thread count. Every failure was a held reply closed to `error` by a sibling test's sweep. A new live test, `a_sweep_under_way_when_a_reply_is_held_leaves_it_alone`, forces that interleaving: the sweep's pool has one connection, checked out, so the sweep waits while the reply is held and goes quiet. It failed 20 of 20 runs on the old sweep and passes 50 of 50 with the fix. With the fix, the same schedules passed 3,600 of 3,600 runs (500 of them the crate's ignored tests at CI's four threads, on the commit as pushed). `bun run gate` passes, with `-p` cargo only. Not run: a booted api; `keep_replies`, which calls the sweep, is unchanged.
