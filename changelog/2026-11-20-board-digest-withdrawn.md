- **The daily board digest is withdrawn before release: a board's notifications come as they happen, and Home's Inbox is the once-a-day view.** Per-board notification settings keep their presets (All activity, Participating, Mentions & assignments, Off), Custom events and scope, and the board's own Alerts and Email. They lose the cadence choice: the bell has no Daily digest entry and no clock mark, and Board settings → Notifications and Settings → Notifications → Boards have no "When" row. `PUT /api/boards/{id}/notify` and `PATCH /api/notifications` refuse `cadence: "digest"` again ("a daily digest isn't available yet"), the registry lists the digest as unavailable, and the `board-digest` feature, scheduler job and notification kind are gone. A digest a testing build stored, on a board's own setting or on the default for every board, is heard as instant: the api reads it that way, and a migration sets each stored one back to instant, keeping the rest of the setting. Without that, the person would hear nothing from that board. The migration also drops the digest's ledger table. The approvals daily digest mail and its Settings switch are unchanged.
