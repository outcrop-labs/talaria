- **Fix: reading a thread's replies now clears the channel's unread badge — the per-thread read cursor.** The bug: the thread panel posted no read at all, and the channel cursor advanced only on the main feed (`useChannelMessages` filters `thread_root_id is null`), so a reply's seq — drawn from the same `channels.msg_seq` counter — could never clear the badge counting it. The fix, mirroring `conversation_reads`: a `channel_thread_reads` table (PK `(thread_root_id, user_id)`, cascading FKs to `channel_messages`/`users`, monotonic `greatest` upsert `mark_channel_thread_read`), a thread arm on `POST /api/channels/{id}/read` — `{ threadRootId, seq? }`, seq computed server-side as the thread's newest complete reply when absent, root normalized `thread_root_id.unwrap_or(root.id)` and 404'd when not the channel's — and the unread predicates in all three spellings (`list_channels`, `channel_unread_total`, `shared_rooms_sql!` — the rail badge, its total, and the profile drawer stay in lockstep) exclude replies a thread cursor covers. The thread's bell rows clear by `group_key` (`reply:<root>`), never by href: a @mention row on the same channel shares the href and must keep waiting. The panel (`ThreadPanel.svelte`) posts the read on mount and as replies land, only while the person is looking (`lib/looking.ts`), the same rule `ChannelView` keeps.
