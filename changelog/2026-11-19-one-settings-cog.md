- **Every settings door in a view wears the same gear and says whose settings it opens.** The board's settings, the list view's column settings and a channel's or group's settings used three different glyphs (two sliders and a gear) with names like "Configure columns" or "Settings: people, agents, rename". They are now one gear from one shared component, named "Board settings", "Column settings", "Channel settings" and "Group settings" in both the tooltip and the accessible name. The column button grew from 24px to 28px without changing the header row's height. The gear brightens on keyboard focus as well as on hover. The notification bell's footer link now reads "Notification settings" and shows the gear. The conversation header's notification button still shows its level (bell, @ or bell-off), is named "Channel notifications: mentions only" (or Group or Direct message), and its menu ends with "Notification settings". In the agents and model providers row menus, "Manage" now shows the gear instead of sliders. Two board menus that put settings next to Delete are split. A saved view gets a "View settings" gear beside its tab, holding its name, "Update to current filters" and Delete. A workchain gets a "Workchain settings" gear beside its switcher, holding its name, a Paused switch and Delete. Right-clicking a saved view's tab still works. Viewers are no longer offered saved-view or workchain controls that the server refuses them. Apps get the same gear from `@talaria/sdk` (`SettingsButton`, `SettingsGlyph`, `cogMenuIcon`, `COG`). A new `bun run check` rule fails any other gear import, the old sliders included.
