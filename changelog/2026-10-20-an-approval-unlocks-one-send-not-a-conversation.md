- **Changed:** Approving an agent's email or calendar chip no longer unlocks that tool for the rest of the conversation. The grant an approval leaves on the conversation (`conversations.unlocked_tools`, now `{tool, expiresAt, usesLeft, recipients}` per tool instead of a bare name) lasts 30 minutes, covers one further call, and — for `gmail_send` and `calendar_create` — only a draft whose to/cc/bcc or attendees are a subset of the people the approved draft named; the next `draft_email` / `draft_calendar_event` spends it once, under a row lock, and answers "Sent — the earlier approval covered this one; the next needs its own." Anything outside the grant queues a fresh card, and the old unbounded string entries grant nothing. A pending Google action is now claimed (`status = 'executing'`) before it runs, so two approvals of the same row inside the Google round-trip — two admins, or the chip and the inbox card — send once and the second sees it already decided; a not-connected outcome hands the row back. `list_pending_sends` / `read_pending_send` show an org agent only the rows it queued, not every org agent's unsent mail. Verified: `talaria-chips` unit tests (grant shape, recipient normalisation, subset rule, one-use consumption, merge), `talaria-google-pending` and `talaria-routes-integrations` unit tests, `cargo fmt` and `cargo clippy -D warnings` on each, and `bun run check`. The live approve path against a real Google account was not exercised in this change.
