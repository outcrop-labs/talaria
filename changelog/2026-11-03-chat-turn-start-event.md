- **Fix: a chat, work, plan or research reply now announces its start on the firehose, not only its end, so the tab bar's agent glyph lights when the reply starts.** `/api/working` already counted a streaming reply, but the app refetched it only on the firehose's `conversation` event, which a reply sent at completion. The bar learned of a start from its 30-second idle poll, so a short reply could come and go with no glyph. The chat route and the queued-turn path now fan a `conversation` event once the streaming reply row exists.
  Verified: `bun run gate`. On a worktree stack with a real agent, an in-page firehose log showed a `conversation` event at each reply's start as well as its end, and the Comms tab's glyph lit 40 ms after send (event at +17 ms) and cleared on the end event 11 s later; before the fix, two turns of 9 and 10 s showed no glyph at all because the tab only learned of them from its 30 s poll.
