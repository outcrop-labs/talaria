- **Fixed:** **An app's API and compiled client now admit only people granted the app, and each app surface is its own grant.** Before, `/api/apps/<slug>/*` checked only the work view `/x/<slug>`, so any member could call a manage-only or settings-only app, and a member granted only the work view could call the routes behind the app's Manage surface. A grant for only an app's Manage surface did nothing: the route gate, the nav and the gateway all denied it as a child of the denied work view. `/app-builds/*` served every build file with no session at all, including the compiled `server.js` and `mcp.js` and the `current.json` that names the build key. Now:
  - The gateway reads the caller's grants from the Rust `GET /api/auth/session` with their own cookie, so team grants count. The host's TypeScript copy of the denied-view resolution, which read only the person's row, is deleted. The gateway admits a caller who holds any of the app's surfaces and gives the app `ctx.access { admin, work, manage, settings }`, `ctx.perms` and `ctx.can(perm)`, so an app can refuse its manage routes to a work-only caller.
  - The work view `/x/<slug>` and the manage view `/x/<slug>/manage` are matched exactly instead of by prefix, in the route gate, the nav and the manage sidebar. A settings tab follows the manage grant. An app that ships settings without a manage surface now has `/x/<slug>/manage` to grant; before, its tab and its server were open to every member.
  - Teams can grant app views: Manage → Teams shows an Apps row. A person's or a team's manage-view list now takes up to 200 views of up to 80 characters. The old limit of 10 refused a member who held every core manage view and any three app views.
  - `/app-builds/` serves only `app.js`, `app.css` and `assets/**`, through the same gate, as `private` cache.

The callers were an admin, a member granted only the work view, a member granted only the manage view, a member granted the manage view through a team, a member granted only the settings-only app, a member with nothing, and no session. Results by caller:
  - **API.** The `whoami` route answered 401 with no session and 403 for the member with nothing. Each admitted caller got the right `ctx.access`. The app's own manage route answered 403 to the work-only member and 200 to the manage-only and team members.
  - **Builds.** `/app-builds/` gave each caller 200, 403 or 401 for `app.js` and `app.css` under the same rule. It returned 404 to everyone for `server.js`, `mcp.js`, `current.json`, `_entry.css` and `..`/`%2e%2e`/`%2f` traversals.
  - **Pages.** In Chromium, the manage-only and team members got the Manage sidebar row and the `/x/<slug>/manage` page, and were bounced from `/x/<slug>`. The work-only member was the reverse. The settings tabs followed the manage grant.
