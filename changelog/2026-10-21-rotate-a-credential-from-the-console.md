- **Added:** **Rotate credential** at the bottom of an agent's Summary tab, for its managers and admins: the console surface for `POST /api/fleet/defs/{id}/rotate-key`. The trigger is a quiet danger link; the deliberate step is an in-place confirm whose copy is checked against what the route does: the old key stops working the moment you confirm (no undo, no grace period); the fleet is re-rendered and a managed agent's container is rolled onto the new key, with the old container locked out until the roll's new container is healthy, so a toolkit call or a workbench session in flight on the old key fails and has to be retried while conversations carry on in the new container; an agent Talaria does not run gets the new key the next time it starts; the audit trail records who rotated which key, never the value; a failed roll leaves the agent on the dead key, and the confirm says how to recover (roll it again, or stop and start it). The result and any failure land in the same place rather than a toast, and the new secret is never shown. `docs/AGENT-KEY-MIGRATION.md` no longer says the route is the only surface.
