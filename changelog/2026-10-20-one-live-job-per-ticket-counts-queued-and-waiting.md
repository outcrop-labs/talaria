- **Fixed:** Two workbench jobs could share one branch. `start_job`'s one-job-per-ticket check only counted `started`, so a second call while the first was still `queued` or `awaiting_approval` minted the same deterministic branch name, "resumed" the first job's branch from a second workdir, and `finish_job` on either opened a PR carrying both. The check now counts `started`, `queued` and `awaiting_approval`. The queue sweep's promotion (`queued → started`) re-asks the per-agent live cap and the per-ticket rule at the moment the job would become a clone and a harness — holding a job that fails either in its place (not blocking the queue behind it) rather than flipping it on rules that were only true when it was filed. And the job sweep gained two conservative staleness rules for what nothing else can close: a `started` or awaiting-approval job with no ticket and no `updated_at` for seven days is abandoned and its workdir removed; a job still `queued` after a day is abandoned (it never had a workdir) and its ticket's board-level wait cleared. Verified: `talaria-workbench-queue` unit tests (the promotion rule at the cap and on a ticket with a started job), `talaria-workbench-mcp` unit tests (the sweep's decision table: closed tickets as before, an open ticket keeping its job however old, a ticketless job at six days left and at seven abandoned, a queued job at a day abandoned and younger left, undated never stale); fmt and clippy `-D warnings` clean on both.
