- **A work session can be rewound to any restorable step of its ledger, as a durable, failable run.** `POST /api/runs/{id}/rewind` (`{seq}`) starts it. The rewind is its own `run-rewind` run: it stops the session, waits until the agent itself reports the turn over or its store holds no turn lease on the session, forks the agent's conversation under the attempt's run id, cuts it after the step's tool result, restores the step's snapshot into a new job checkout on a branch with an `-aN` suffix, verifies the conversation and the tree, and then inserts the next numbered attempt, which continues from that turn with its budget carried. A rewind that fails at any stage is a failed `run-rewind` row with its reason and never an empty or finished attempt; a retry after a crash converges on the same end state and touches nothing that happened since, so an attempt that already exists is never retired or discarded by a later try; the ticket is read again under the lock before the attempt is inserted, so a ticket a person closed in the meantime fails the rewind instead of being reopened; an attempt whose ticket cannot take the work fails with the reason instead of finishing with zero turns. A ticket in review moves back to its working column as the requesting person's action; a done ticket is refused. The dispatcher now takes a per-ticket-and-agent lock around its generation walk and stands down while a rewind holds the pair. Verified: 22 cases against one stateful fake world drive every stage, a crash right after each stage with the retry resuming from the persisted checkpoint, a retried start beside a newer attempt, every refusal and the tree and conversation mismatches (`cargo test -p talaria-runs-rewind`); `scripts/rewind-proof.mjs` prints the lineage's attempts and the rewinds that name a run.
