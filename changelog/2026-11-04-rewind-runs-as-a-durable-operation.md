- **A work session can be rewound to any restorable step of its ledger, as a durable, failable run.** `POST /api/runs/{id}/rewind` (`{seq}`) starts it. The rewind is its own `run-rewind` run: it stops the session, waits for the agent to be quiet, forks the agent's conversation under the attempt's run id, cuts it after the step's tool result, restores the step's snapshot into a new job checkout on a branch with an `-aN` suffix, verifies the conversation and the tree, and then inserts the next numbered attempt, which continues from that turn with its budget carried. A rewind that fails at any stage is a failed `run-rewind` row with its reason and never an empty or finished attempt; an attempt whose ticket cannot take the work fails with the reason instead of finishing with zero turns. A ticket in review moves back to its working column as the requesting person's action; a done ticket is refused. The dispatcher now takes a per-ticket-and-agent lock around its generation walk and stands down while a rewind holds the pair. Verified: 19 fake-world cases drive every stage, a crash after each stage, every refusal and the tree and conversation mismatches (`cargo test -p talaria-runs-rewind`); `scripts/rewind-proof.mjs` prints the lineage's attempts and the rewinds that name a run.
