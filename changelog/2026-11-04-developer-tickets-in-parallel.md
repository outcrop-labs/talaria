- **Fixed: a Developer Agent's tickets progress in parallel instead of one at a time behind a single long build.** Talaria and Hermes already ran each ticket's session concurrently. The bottleneck was the agent's own container. Every ticket's clone, build, test suite and Oh My Pi run executes inside it, and a chassis seeded from an older template still gives it `cpus: ${AGENT_CPUS:-2}`, so up to 16 tickets' builds shared two cores. On the owner's instance three tickets ran on those two cores at a load average of 15, on a 32-core host. A cold `cargo build` ran past Hermes' 420-second tool ceiling, was killed and started again from cold, and two of the three tickets spent 40 minutes without reaching their harness. A developer agent's service now drops an inherited hard CPU quota (`cpus`, `cpu_quota`, `cpu_period`) and keeps its `cpu_shares`. Its config now sets Hermes' `gateway.api_server.max_concurrent_runs` to the live-job cap plus 8, which is 24. Hermes' default of 10 counts chats and crons too, and would have refused the eleventh ticket's turn with a 429. The Oh My Pi skill now says to run steps that may pass five minutes in the background and wait on them with `process_manage`, never through the foreground ceiling. Each job gets its own `--session-dir`. Applies to any repository a workbench runs.

  Verified: render unit tests cover the quota strip and the run cap (a def's larger cap, or 0, stands), and the whole `talaria-fleet-render` suite passes. On a worktree stack whose chassis carries outcrop's `cpus: ${AGENT_CPUS:-2}`, the rendered Developer Agent (Hermes v0.22.4) came up with `NanoCpus=0` and `max_concurrent_runs: 24` in its config. Three tickets assigned to it were dispatched in the same second, and the agent ran their steps at the same time. Six live tickets then ran a stand-in build (two CPU-bound jobs each) every turn. Uncapped, each build took 8–11 s. After `docker update --cpus 2`, which is the quota the old render kept, the same builds took 46–55 s. Once the quota was lifted they were back to 8–10 s. All 335 of the agent's model calls were booked to their tickets.
