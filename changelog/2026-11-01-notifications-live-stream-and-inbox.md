- **Changed: the live stream resumes and the inbox gains an API — one Redis subscriber per api process instead of one per open stream; a reconnecting stream replays every notification it missed, by seq, each exactly once; a stream ends when its session does (sign-out, a deleted or expired session); the api stops within about a second with streams open; and `GET`/`PUT /api/notifications` gain views, keyset pages, filters, a `since` read, Done (archive) and mark-unread.** The server half of the second round of notification work (docs/notifications/live-and-inbox.md). Every event stream — the person's own, boards, channels, runs and the run-watch tail — now rides one subscriber connection per process (`talaria-realtime`'s hub): a topic is subscribed while any stream wants it and unsubscribed the moment the last one leaves, a stream that falls 256 messages behind ends (its client reconnects), and when the connection drops the hub reconnects, resubscribes and tells every user stream to resync. On `/api/me/events`, a notification frame carries its seq as the SSE `id:`; a reopen with `?since=` or `Last-Event-ID` sends the person's presence, then every row after that seq (up to 100, else a "read it yourself" resync), then a resync frame, and drops the live copies of what it replayed. Logout publishes the session on `session:revoked` and every stream it opened ends at once with a `session` frame; every live frame is sent only after its session is re-read, and a quiet stream re-reads it every 20 s. `GET /api/notifications` takes `view=foryou|all|done`, `unread`, `category`, `class`, `kind`, `limit` and `before` (an opaque cursor; `next` in the answer), or `since=<seq>` (rows in seq order with `more`); with no parameters it answers exactly as before, plus `more`. `PUT` takes `entity` beside `ids` and `href`, and `archive`, `unarchive` or `unread`: Done also marks read, so the bell, the Home Inbox, the brief and the badges drop a Done row without a change of their own, and every path tells the person's other tabs (`readIds` for a read or a Done, the counts otherwise). The Home Inbox's mark-read and its undo now tell the other tabs too. Reading a row cancels its pending deferred push, and marking it unread never brings that push back or mails it at its old due time: a pending mail is re-armed from the reopen, at the person's own frequency (a row already mailed stays mailed). `unread` leaves Done rows out, so it can no longer disagree with `unreadForYou`, and `/api/unreads`' research badge leaves them out too; a crafted page cursor is a 400 rather than a 500. On shutdown the hub closes with the streams, in-flight responses get at most 5 s, the pool 1 s, and each drain phase logs how long it took — which named the old linger: 3 of 10 stops with streams open took 11–35 s, every one waiting on a single boot-time seed query held behind a slow disk. New `features`: `since`, `inbox-v2`, `sse-replay`, `archive`. Nothing older breaks: the shell's watcher, older pages and the Kotlin client ignore the new frames and fields.
