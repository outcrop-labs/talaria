- **Changed: a loading skeleton keeps its motion and its pixels, and the page clock sleeps between its 8 Hz ticks instead of running 60 frames a second (UB6).** The skeleton's static re-rolls 8 times a second, but it filtered every frame of the shared page clock to find those 8, so one skeleton cost 60 animation frames a second. `subscribeToClock(fn, everyMs)` in `lib/motion.ts` makes a subscription periodic: it runs on the first frame past each multiple of `everyMs`, and while only periodic subscribers are left the clock sleeps on a timer and wakes one frame early for the boundary.
