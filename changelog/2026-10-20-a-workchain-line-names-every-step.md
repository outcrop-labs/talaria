- **Fixed:** A workchain `positions` PATCH naming only some steps silently un-gated the rest (no predecessor reads as the head, and the heartbeat served them to agents at once), two concurrent wires could land a 2-cycle because the cycle walk and the insert were separate statements, and a repeated `taskId` stored a self-edge. The PATCH now refuses a line that does not name every step exactly once, the edge POST checks and inserts under the chain's row lock in one transaction, and the table carries `CHECK (from_step <> to_step)` (existing self-edges are deleted by the migration). A workflow PUT is one transaction. Verified: `cargo test -p talaria-routes-boards -p talaria-workflows`, clippy `-D warnings` on both, `bun run migrations:check` with the snapshot matching.
