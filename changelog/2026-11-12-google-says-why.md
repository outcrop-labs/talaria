- **When Google refuses, agents, approvers and admins now see Google's own words, quoted, where they used to see "not connected" or "Could not reach Google".** Google's status and body were always on the failure, and `upstream_reason` (talaria-google-errors) now lifts them out. A REST body gives its most specific code (the `ErrorInfo` reason, else `status`, else the legacy `errors[].reason`) and then its message. An OAuth body gives `error` and then `error_description`. The quote is capped at 300 characters, code first. A Google call that fails answers `Google <Product> refused: «…»`, and a scope refusal adds `Google said: «…»` to its reconnect sentence. An agent's own token used to be swallowed: `resolve_agent_google` called `.unwrap_or_default()`, so every agent route read a revoked grant or a bad client secret as "No Google account is connected for this agent". It now returns the error. `invalid_grant` answers 409 `reconnect_needed` and says the grant was cleared. `invalid_client` and `unauthorized_client`, the new `TokenError::ClientRejected`, answer 502 `client_rejected`. That class keeps the stored grant and names the admin's fix. The PR only classifies it; flagging the row is later work. When the token fails at approval, the held action goes back to `pending` with the quoted reason. Before, it stayed `executing` for ten minutes and the approver read "decide failed". The agent's queue reads it as `lastError`. A failed action quotes Google instead of the fixed "Google rejected the action." The three connect bounces carry Google's OAuth code as `&reason=` (`invalid_client`, `invalid_grant`, `access_denied`). The two flashes show it, and the Agents page now reads its `googleAgent` bounce; nothing read that bounce before. Nothing pattern-matches a "not agentic" refusal, because no such body has been captured. docs/GOOGLE-WORKSPACE.md has a new section, "When Google refuses".
