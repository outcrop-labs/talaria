- **Fixed: Talaria Desktop always has a way back when an instance's page does not come up.** While an instance restarts behind Cloudflare, its page could hang for 100 seconds and end on Cloudflare's 524, show a 502 or a challenge, or, on Windows, WebView2's network error page — in a window with no close, minimize or drag, because the styled titlebar is drawn by the page. Now a page on the instance's own address that is not Talaria puts you back on the launcher with a card saying what answered. A page that answered (an error page, a Cloudflare challenge, a sign-in gate) stays alive behind the card: **Show the page** shows it as it is, with **← Instances** on it as the way back; Retry and Back to instances close it. A page that has not loaded in 25 seconds gets "<instance> is not answering", with Keep waiting. If Talaria comes up while a card is up, it opens by itself. A page with Talaria up is never interrupted, older Talaria versions included (their titlebar's call counts, and a page still starting is looked at again about 8 and 20 seconds after it loads); pages on other addresses are never judged; Retry opens the instance's home, not the page that failed; and an instance loaded in the background at launch that does not come up is tried again later.
  Verified: on Linux, on a private headless rig (WebDriver on the launcher, fake instances whose beacon answers so the open reaches the webview), against this change's first round. Main's real UI build with its lazy Tauri chunk 10 s late: opened, no card (the first round closed it at 3.9 s); 30 s late: the card at 21.5 s with the page kept, then opened by itself at 31.0 s (the first round closed it for good). Held 100 s, then 524: the not-answering card at 25.2 s, then the 524 card at 103.9 s with the page kept. A Cloudflare-style challenge that passes by itself: the 403 card at 4.1 s, then opened by itself at 7.4 s (the first round closed it). A challenge that wants a click, under a strict CSP: Show the page, ← Instances back to the card, Show the page again, the click, Talaria up and the way back gone. A same-origin sign-in gate: the card, Show the page, sign in, Talaria. A share page as the last page: Retry opened the root (the first round reopened the share page every time). A 502: the card at 4.1 s, the page kept; a dropped connection: the card, the page closed; Retry three times on a 502: one page at a time, then open once it answered; pages on another origin: no card; Keep waiting on a file shown after a stall: no card again; a page loaded at launch that hung or answered 502: closed with no card, loaded again a minute later. Unit tests cover each rule and every row of the decision after a page finishes. Not verified: Windows and macOS at run time, a real instance behind Cloudflare.
