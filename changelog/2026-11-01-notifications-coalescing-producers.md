- **Changed: notifications are one row per thing with a count — a DM, a thread, a ticket's status burst, a flapping MCP server, an approval and its reminders each keep one row that updates — the people taking part in a ticket or a thread are told, every channel and DM gets a level (All / Mentions / Nothing) and a timed mute from its header, an approval's rows clear for everyone the moment someone decides it, and a plan @mention reaches the person again.** The producers half of the second round of notification work (docs/notifications/producers.md). A DM coalesces every message into one row per conversation (the newest line, the count, a re-alert), a row filed before the contract is adopted with its id, and a read row means the next message files a fresh one. An agent's replies in your thread, its daily outreach, a ticket's comments and a channel thread's replies do the same; a ticket's status moves within ten minutes are one quiet row ("Moved 4 times — now in review") that alerts once, and the ticket's creator now hears them. A comment on a ticket reaches its assignees, creator, watchers and earlier commenters, and a thread reply reaches the root's author and earlier repliers, each re-checked for access when the row is filed, so someone removed from a board gets nothing. Levels: a channel defaults to Mentions, a DM to All; All files one counting row per channel; Nothing silences @mentions too; a mute lands rows in the bell without a toast, banner or push. The header's menu sets them and says "Muted until …"; a person who reaches a channel only through a team cannot set a level yet (an owner decision). Approvals and their SLA reminders share one row per person, so a nag re-alerts it, and a decision reads every recipient's row with the read-sync ids; an MCP server's recovery reads every admin's down row, and a server going down again within the hour reopens it with the count. Every row now names who caused it and what it is about. A plan @mention had matched nobody whose email was not their first name, because the candidate list swapped name and email, and it read each person's teams one query at a time; both fixed. A caller that hangs up right after a ticket edit or a channel post no longer loses the notifications the write owed. A post with no text — a file on its own — rings the other side of a DM (and a channel at All) as a line of text does, the row reading "Sent an attachment"; the header's "Muted until …" follows the conversation's name and gives way first in a narrow header, and a change made on another device shows on the next refetch.
