- **Changed: in Comms, one click on an agent opens a blank conversation with it, and every row in the sidebar has a menu of its own actions.** A click (or Enter) on an agent's name opens the fresh composer addressed to that agent and lists its recent threads under it. No thread is created until the first message is sent. The "new conversation" button stays, and a chevron over the avatar (or Right/Left) folds the threads away. Right-click, the Menu key, Shift+F10, a long-press on a touch screen, or the row's "…" button opens the row's menu, and the menu shows only what the person may do. A thread, channel, relay or DM offers Open, Copy link, Open in new tab (Open in browser on Talaria Desktop), Mark read or Mark unread, Pin, and for a channel or DM a Notifications submenu with levels and timed mutes. The owner also gets Rename, Archive and Delete. An agent offers New conversation, Copy link, View profile, Agent settings (for its managers), Pin, and Mute DMs for 1, 8 or 24 hours or until turned back on. Pins now live on the server (`/api/me/pins`), so they follow the person to every browser and to Talaria Desktop, and the rail's "Starred" section is now "Pinned". Stars already saved in a browser are moved to the server the first time that browser loads Comms. Three new endpoints back the rest: `POST /api/conversations/{id}/unread` and `POST /api/channels/{id}/unread` move the person's own read marker back one message, and `/api/me/agent-mutes` stores a timed mute of one agent's replies and check-ins. While a mute holds, those rows still reach the bell, but quietly: no toast, push or mail. The menu itself (`ui/ContextMenu`) gained keyboard support: arrows, Home/End, typeahead, Right/Left through a submenu, and Esc or Tab to close, with focus returning to the row. It also gained a long-press on touch and placement that keeps the menu and its submenu inside the viewport. It also no longer removes a dialog that one of its items had just opened.
