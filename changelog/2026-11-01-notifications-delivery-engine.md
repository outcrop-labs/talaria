- **Changed: notifications now reach a person once, on the device they are using, in the channel they chose — the server enforces the class × channel matrix, DND and working hours with a Needs-you break-through, holds a push for a minute while one of the person's clients is in use (cancelled when a client acks the row or the person reads it), batches missed-activity mail into one message per person, keeps "work finished" and paused workchains quiet by default while a channel you set to All alerts like a direct message, and hardens the push edge; the per-device test push works.** The delivery engine of the second round of notification work (docs/notifications/delivery.md). Every write now decides, inside its own transaction, from the class's Desktop, Push and Email channels, the person's DND and working hours (in their profile's time zone, or the workspace's when the profile has none — the zone their brief opens in, as Profile's "Workspace default" says; it used to be UTC), what the producer said, the instance switches (`emailEnabled`, and a new `pushEnabled` that defaults on) and whether one of their clients is looking or active. A row held by a pause or outside working hours is a feed row, is not pushed, and mails when the pause ends; Needs-you classes still banner and push unless the person turns that off. With a client in use, the push waits 60 s and a 5-second job sends it unless a client acked the row or it was read; the write reads presence before it locks the person's row, and treats a Redis failure as "nobody there" and pushes. Mail is no longer queued in memory and sent within 30 s: the row carries its due time (15 minutes for Needs you, else the person's frequency — 15 minutes, an hour, or 09:00 next on their clock), and a one-minute job sends each person ONE mail with every row of theirs still unread ("N unread in Talaria", grouped by category, through the same kill switch as the digest), mails a conversation that was already mailed again at the person's frequency while it stays unread (its next message re-arms it; a mail already pending stands, so a burst is still one mail), retries a failure after 5 and 15 minutes, gives up on the third, and drops due rows rather than holding them while mail is switched off; the legacy email-only route still files the row read once a provider takes the mail. Work finished and workchains paused (Following) default to the feed only; a stored route or channel still wins, so nobody who chose banners loses them. Channel activity, the one row a channel set to All files (only once the person picks All; a channel starts at Mentions), banners, pushes and mails like a direct message by default, and stays in the All tab, off the For-you badge. A new `account` class carries the notice that an agent now acts as you on Google (it used to fall into the quiet bucket). Pushes carry Urgency and TTL by category (Needs you: high, 24 h; direct: normal, 12 h; news: low, 1 h) and the row id as `Topic`, so a re-alert replaces an undelivered push. The VAPID key is unsealed once per key instead of on every delivery, the plane and the batcher read the live secretbox so a key rotation (which now re-seals `push_vapid` too) never breaks push or mail until a restart, a timeout is retried once instead of twice, a device that timed out or failed three times is skipped for 2^failures minutes (an hour at most) while the person's other devices are served at once, and ten failures in a row with no delivery and no re-subscribe for a week prune it. Sessions now write a liveness marker beside themselves, and a daily job deletes the push subscriptions of sessions that ended (logout or the 7-day expiry), after first marking every live session that predates the marker; another daily job deletes read notifications after 90 days and unread ones 180 days after their last activity, with their inbox-focus rows. The presence winner cache is ordered by the read each heartbeat makes (one atomic script and a counter), so two racing heartbeats can no longer leave the older winner cached with no `presence` frame for the newer one. `features` adds `email-batch`, `quiet-following`, `dnd-enforced` and `presence-gated-push`.
