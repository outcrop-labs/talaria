- **A ticket moves between boards — for people from the ticket and the card menu, and for agents through `move_ticket`.** `POST /api/tasks/{id}/move { boardId, status?, preview? }` re-keys the ticket on the destination's counter and keeps its old ref resolving (`task_ref_aliases`, so `PLAT-118` in a doc or an agent's memory still opens it). The column maps by the caller's choice, else the same name, else the same category, else the destination's intake — and a move never closes an open ticket. Labels the destination lacks are dropped, never created there; a person or agent who is not on the destination is unassigned; a sub-task leaves its parent behind; a reviewer's sign-off does not travel; watchers who cannot see the destination go dormant. Each is named in the answer's `moved`, on the ticket's activity line, and in the audit log (`task.board_move`). A person needs edit rights on both boards. An agent needs board policy on both and keeps its HITL stops: no closed ticket, no ticket in review or blocked, never into a closed column or a pickup column it was not already in. A live work session, a workchain step, or a ticket with sub-tasks refuses with 409. Both boards' views update live; the ticket and its comments are re-indexed under the new board; the destination's watchers, assignees and creator are told, nobody else. Old `/boards/<old>/<ticket>` links follow the ticket to its new board. The "Move to board…" dialog previews what changes before the move.
  Verified: `bun run gate`; `cargo test -p talaria-tasks moving` (the status, label and summary mappings: name → category → intake, never a closed column for an open ticket, an agent never into a closed or pickup column and never out of review or blocked), the sandbox and chips tests, `bun run test -- ticket-move`, and four live-DB tests (`task_move_live::` — the permission matrix for people and agents, the done refusals, the re-key with the old ref resolving, the work-session hold). In the running app on a worktree stack: a person moved PLAT-1 from Platform to Support Desk (renamed columns, different labels) through Move to board…; the dialog said PLAT-1 → SUP-2, In Progress by name, `urgent` dropped, Bo unassigned and his watch going quiet, and offered no Shipped column; the ticket landed as SUP-2 with `Billing` kept, the source board's open tab lost the card and the destination's gained it without a reload, the old /boards/<Platform>/<ticket> link followed it to Support Desk, and the activity line named both refs and what was dropped. An agent with its own key, through the real toolkit over stdio, saw move_ticket in tools/list, previewed and moved PLAT-2 → SUP-1, and was refused for a board it is not on and for `shipped` and `cancelled`; GET /api/tasks/PLAT-2 opens SUP-1 for the person and the agent. The 400s (a done column for an open ticket, a key the board lacks) and 409s (a sub-task parent, a live work session) answered as designed, a sub-task moved without its parent, the audit log carried `task.board_move`, and only the destination's creator was notified (not the mover, not the watcher who cannot see it).
