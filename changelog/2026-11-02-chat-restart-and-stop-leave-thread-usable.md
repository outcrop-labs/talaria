- **Fixed: a restart or a stop leaves the chat thread usable.** A reply cut off by an api restart no longer holds its thread for 15 minutes: each process stamps the replies it writes every 10 s, and a `streaming` reply nobody has stamped for 120 s (450 s for one nothing has written since it was made, which a container from before this release may still be waiting on) is closed at boot or on the next beat, its words kept and the reason after them. A message sent after the restart is answered once the reply is closed; one queued before the restart is answered by the thread's next send. A stop before the model spoke no longer saves Hermes' "Operation interrupted…" notice as the reply, and a stopped turn files no "replied" notification. A stop Hermes could not place inside its 10 s window is sent again until it lands (`held: true`) instead of a 502 the page swallowed; a stop the agent refuses still answers 502, and the page now says when a stop is held or did not reach the agent.

  Verified: on the rel-fleet stack (api :5407, Postgres :5607) against a mock Hermes API server that mirrors v0.22.4's completions wire. On rc 65dbae86: a stop before the first token stored "Operation interrupted: waiting for model response (2.8s elapsed)." and filed "Rfops replied"; a stop Hermes answered `409 not_found` for 20 s got a 502 at 9.6 s and the agent finished; after a `kill -9` mid-reply and a restart, the follow-up answered 202 and was still unanswered 3 min 25 s later. On this branch: the same restart closed the reply 120 s after its last stamp and answered the follow-up a second later; with the api down 130 s, boot closed it and the next send ran at once; a turn that sent no byte for 150 s stayed alive; the early stop landed empty and stopped with no notification; the unplaced stop answered `held` at 9.3 s and landed at 22.9 s. Live tests `orphan_live` (8, four runs) and `stop_landing_live` (3) fail against rc's code and pass here, including a reply nothing has written since it was made surviving the sweep at 200 s and closing at 500 s, and a revived orphan landing without its `orphaned` stamp; unit tests pin Hermes' exact notice shapes (a reply that merely begins "Operation interrupted" keeps its words), the stop classifier (only `409 not_found`, `503 storage_unavailable` or no answer are sent again), and one patient sender per turn.
