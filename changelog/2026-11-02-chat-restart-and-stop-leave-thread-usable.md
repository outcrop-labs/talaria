- **Fixed: a restart or a stop leaves the chat thread usable — a reply cut off by a restart no longer holds its thread for 15 minutes, a stop before the model spoke no longer saves Hermes' "Operation interrupted…" as the reply or rings "replied", and a stop the agent cannot take inside its 10 s window is sent again until it lands instead of failing silently.** An api restart in the middle of a reply (a deploy update, a container restart, an OOM) left its row `streaming` with no writer, and the thread waited fifteen minutes of silence to call it dead: the next message answered 202 queued and was never run, and Stop answered ok and changed nothing. Each process now holds the replies it writes and stamps them alive every 10 s, quiet stretches included; a `streaming` reply nobody has stamped for 120 s is closed as an error that keeps its words and says the reply stopped being saved partway, most likely in a restart. The api sweeps those at boot and every 10 s after, and a message queued behind a reply this process is not writing is answered as soon as that reply turns out to have no writer. The lease, not "not mine", decides, so the old container of a deploy's overlap keeps its live replies. A turn stopped before its first token used to land with Hermes' own cancellation notice as its content (shown as the agent's reply on reload, sent back to the model as its earlier answer, and filed as "Chatbot replied: Operation interrupted…"); it now lands empty and stopped, and no stopped turn files a reply notification. A completions-arm stop Hermes answered `409 not_found` for the whole window (it has not bound the turn yet, which a starved agent store has taken minutes to do) answered 502, which the page swallowed while the agent worked, and spent, to the end; it now goes to the same patient sender a held stop gets and answers `held: true`, and the page says when a stop has not reached the agent yet or never did.
