- **Due-date reminders, off until you choose them — per-board notification settings, part 4.** A new `task-due` job runs every 15 minutes and reminds at two points of a ticket's due date: once when it is due within a day ("<ticket>: due soon") and once when it goes overdue ("<ticket>: overdue"), into the same notification, which alerts again. Only people whose setting for the board names due dates hear them — All activity, or a custom set holding "Due soon or overdue" (`PUT /api/boards/{id}/notify`; the event was listed but refused until now) — so with no setting nobody is told, and a watch turns nothing new on. The ticket's watchers, assignees and creator get a `task-due` notification in "Threads you're in" (banner and push by default); people who hear the whole board get it on the board's activity row. Each point fires once per due date (`tasks.due_soon_for` and `tasks.overdue_for` record it, and moving the due date re-arms both), never for a ticket already a day past due — opting in does not replay a backlog — and never for archived, done, failed or cancelled tickets. A ticket that passes a point while nobody on its board listens is decided all the same, so a setting changed later applies from the next point on. Feature `task-due`; the board's own control arrives with part 3. Verified by CI on this PR's checks: unit tests for the thresholds (arm, once per due date, re-arm on a move, overdue-only when first seen past due, nothing a day past), the probe pinned to the resolver for every setting, the resolver's due cases, the class and group-key spellings and the required job table; and a new live module, `task_due_live`, driving settings through the real router and running the job's own pass: nobody told without a setting (a self-made watch included) yet both points marked, and opting in afterwards replays nothing; a custom set over my tickets told once per point (due soon, then overdue into the same row with count 2, then due soon again after the date moved) and nobody else; closed, archived and long-overdue tickets skipped, a closed one left unmarked and reminded once reopened; a whole-board listener's own ticket on its own row and someone else's on the board's activity row; a member who opted in and left the board told nothing.
