- **In Talaria Desktop with the themed titlebar, the tab bar is the window's titlebar: it holds the minimize, maximize and close buttons, and dragging its empty space moves the window.** The separate 36px band above it is gone, so every view starts 36px higher. The controls sit at the bar's left on macOS, before the mark, and at its right after the account chip everywhere else. A double-click on empty bar space maximizes the window; a press on a tab, a tile or any other control does only what it does in a browser. Sign-in and the wait for the session have no tab bar and keep the band. A browser, and the OS titlebar setting, show the bar unchanged.
  Verified: `bun run test -- titlebar` (empty bar space drags and maximizes; a press inside a link or a button, a right-click, the OS titlebar setting and a browser do not).
