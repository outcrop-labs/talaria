- **A board owner connects a ClickUp list from Board settings, and everyone on the board sees how the sync is doing (part 2 of 2: the UI).** Board settings gains a **sync** tab. A board owner pastes a ClickUp personal token, picks the list one ClickUp level at a time, and reads a preview before anything is written. The preview shows the status mapping, with only exact name matches filled in and a line under each row saying when the column starts agent work, closes the ticket, or is review or Blocked. It also shows who would and would not be assigned and why, the first tasks as they would land under the mapping being edited, the new labels, oversized files and the first import's time. **Connect and start syncing** runs the first sync.
  - **The connected tab.** It shows the list and the ClickUp member the token acts as (the token's last four characters only), last and next sync, the run's phase, warnings, and the current problems, each with its fix, its ticket and its ClickUp link. Owners edit the mapping (saving re-places what the old mapping placed), what carries over, the schedule, pause and resume (pausing holds only the schedule, as the engine does: Sync now and Re-sync still run), **Replace credential** and **Disconnect**; an editor reads it and presses Sync now.
  - **The board's ClickUp chip.** It sits beside the gear for every member and is never silently stale. It says "synced 12m ago", "syncing", "paused", "sync overdue", "needs a new token", "list not found" or "last sync failed", with the open-problem count; on an instance with scheduled syncs switched off it says that instead of "sync overdue". It opens last/next sync, the problems, Sync now (owners and editors) and **Sync settings** (owners and editors; the modal stays closed to viewers).
  - **Synced tickets.** The header links the ClickUp task and says "opened by ClickUp sync". Owners and editors get **Re-sync from ClickUp**. Cards, list rows and the ticket say when the task was archived, deleted, moved or hidden in ClickUp.
  - **Imported comments** show their ClickUp author's name with a ClickUp badge. They are never resolved against Talaria accounts, never anyone's own message, and have no profile link.
  - **A deep link to Board settings.** `?settings=<tab>` on the board route opens the modal on that tab.
