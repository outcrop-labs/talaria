- **Workbench jobs now tear themselves down.** Nothing ever reclaimed a job's workdir (`/opt/data/workbench/jobs/<id>`): finish_job opened the PR, abandon flipped a status, and a ticket closing under a live job did neither. So each job kept a full checkout plus a cold cargo `target/`, and whatever the agent was still running in it. On the dogfood box one agent held 30 workdirs (78 GiB) and seven concurrent cargo trees, enough to pin the VM's disk and the hypervisor behind it. Now finish_job stops every process in the workdir and keeps the checkout for a revise bounce. Abandon removes the workdir. A new `workbench-job-sweep` (every 10 min) abandons any `started` or awaiting-approval job whose ticket reached a done column or was archived, and removes the workdirs of abandoned jobs and of `pr_open` jobs on closed tickets. Stale jobs also stop holding RAM reservation. New column `workbench_jobs.workspace_cleared_at`.
