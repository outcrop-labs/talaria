- **Fixed: the second message of a conversation no longer buzzes your phone a minute later while you are reading it at your desk, and a Mac that resumes straight into an instance now asks for notification permission there.** A re-alert (a second DM folded into the same notification) is a new version of the row: the server forgets the first version's ack and arms a new deferred push. The page, in a browser tab and in Talaria Desktop alike, acked each notification id only once per page, so the re-alert's toast or banner was never acked and the push went out 60 s later. The page now acks each version (the row id at its `seq`, or its `createdAt` on a row from before the contract). On macOS, nothing can be shown until the person allows Talaria, and the shell asks only from a click. Until now that click lived only on the launcher's card and in Settings → Desktop. Someone who upgraded from 0.11, or relaunched after adding an instance, resumes straight into the instance and never saw either: every banner was refused, and nothing said why. While the shell reports "not determined", the instance now shows a card (once the page has been open 10 s and you have clicked or typed in it) whose Allow button runs the macOS prompt. Don't Allow says where to turn it on later, and Not now is remembered for that instance. Linux and Windows have no such prompt and never show the card.
