- **Added:** Three ways into a session and one way out of it. A "/" at the start of a line in an agent-facing composer opens a command menu over the skills every agent ships with and over your blueprints — prompts you keep to send again; picking a skill inserts the command, which travels to the agent as "Use your `name` skill. …" (what you sent is the sentence, and so is what the transcript shows); picking a blueprint fills the well with it; "Save as blueprint" keeps the draft you are holding, and a blueprint of your own can be removed from the menu. Blueprints are personal, or shared org-wide by whoever may manage templates. An empty session stage offers starter cards — plan a sprint, research a question, draft tickets from a discussion, put the agent on a clock — that start a session with the chosen agent. An Export action in the session header turns the transcript into a document artifact (turns, who spoke, stamps, attachments, secrets scrubbed, a message's own headings never read as structure) with a Word download beside it, and the artifact surfaces offer Download as Word or Excel for any document or sheet.
