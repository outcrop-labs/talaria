# LLM wiki (Talaria)

Do not build a parallel markdown wiki. Company knowledge is already a
knowledgebase:

- Find — `search_knowledge`, then `read_kb_doc`
- Add — `create_kb_doc` in the right space (`list_kb_spaces` first)
- Structure — `move_kb_doc`, `edit_kb_space` for a space landing page

Drafts stay unofficial until a human marks them official. The playbook is the
**talaria-toolkit** skill.
